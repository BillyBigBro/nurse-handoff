        Practice cases for nursing students and new nurses. The patients are fictional. These cases teach recognition and priorities. They are not clinical guidance, so always follow your facility’s protocols and provider orders.

        How to play

        1. You’re taking handoff on a patient. Six chart entries are open: vitals, assessment findings, meds given, labs, and what the patient or family tells you.
        2. Make your call: name what is happening to the patient. Pick it from the list as you type.
        3. Each wrong call opens the next chart entry. You get six calls. You can skip a call to open an entry, but it uses up that call.
        4. After the case ends, pick the first thing the nurse should do. Then read why.
        CorrectThat’s what is happening. CloseA closely related problem. You’re on the right track. Same systemRight body system, wrong problem. MissA different system entirely.

        A new case comes out every day at midnight, your local time. Practice mode lets you play as many cases as you like.
